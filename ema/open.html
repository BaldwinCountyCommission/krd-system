About This Kiosk

This is a shared disaster guest laptop managed by the Baldwin County Emergency Management Agency. It is configured as a restricted kiosk and does not have access to locally installed productivity applications such as Microsoft Word, Excel, or other desktop software.

To create or edit documents, spreadsheets, and presentations, please use a web-based application:

• Microsoft Office Online — visit office.com and sign in with your Microsoft or organizational account to access Word, Excel, PowerPoint, and OneDrive.

• Google Workspace — visit drive.google.com and sign in with your Google account to access Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Drive storage.

Use whichever platform your organization supports. Files saved to OneDrive or Google Drive will remain accessible from your own devices after this session ends.

🔒 When you are finished, use the End Session button on the main screen to immediately log out of all accounts, clear browsing history and cookies, and return this machine to a clean state for the next user.
